If your SOC 2 auditor is A-LIGN, your audit requests live in A-SCEND, A-LIGN's
audit platform. Each request has an ID, such as `R-1105` or `P-18`, and expects
evidence attached to it. Without a connection, someone on your team downloads
each file from wherever it lives and uploads it again, one request at a time.

With the connection set up, your soc2doc expert uploads the evidence onto the
matching A-SCEND requests for you.

## How it works

- **Your expert runs it.** This is expert-in-the-loop work, not a self-serve
  button. Your soc2doc expert decides what goes to which request and runs the
  upload.
- **Upload is not submit.** Uploading a file does not change a request's status
  in A-SCEND. Your team or your expert submits the request when it is ready.
- **A-SCEND stays the auditor's system of record.** soc2doc prepares and
  delivers the evidence; your auditor still works in A-SCEND.

## What soc2doc sends

- **Only approved, in-period evidence.** A file goes to A-SCEND only if it has
  been approved in soc2doc and falls within your audit period.
- **Each file once.** Files are deduplicated, so the same document is not sent
  twice.
- **Every upload is recorded.** For each upload you can see the file, the
  request it went to, when it was sent, and who sent it, so you always know
  what reached your auditor.

<Note>
A-SCEND does not accept files over 100 MB. That limit is A-LIGN's, not ours.
For a larger file, your auditor provides another way to deliver it.
</Note>

## Setup

<Warning>
Only a Company Administrator in A-SCEND can create an API key. Anyone else who
tries gets an error, so ask your Company Administrator to do steps 1 and 2.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key in A-SCEND">
    In A-SCEND, open **Settings > API Keys** and select **Create API Key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it so you can recognize it">
    Give the key a name you will recognize later, such as "soc2doc push", and
    add a note on what it is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the key with your expert securely">
    Send the key and its secret to your soc2doc expert through a secure channel
    you agree on together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a one-file test">
    Choose a request. Your expert uploads a single file to it, and you confirm
    you can see it in A-SCEND. After that, uploads run as normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Never send the key's secret by plain email or chat. Agree on a secure channel
with your expert first.
</Warning>

## You stay in control

The key acts only within your own A-SCEND account. You can regenerate or delete
it at any time in A-SCEND at **Settings > API Keys**. Doing so stops all uploads
from soc2doc immediately. To resume, create a new key and share it with your
expert the same way.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does A-LIGN need to do anything?">
    No. The connection uses A-SCEND's own Open API, which A-SCEND makes
    available to its clients and partners. Your Company Administrator creates
    the key; nothing is needed from your auditor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this replace A-SCEND?">
    No. A-SCEND remains your auditor's system of record. soc2doc is the expert
    layer that prepares the evidence and delivers it there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What about populations and samples?">
    A population uploads like any other evidence. Once your auditor accepts a
    population, they create sample requests in A-SCEND, and soc2doc answers
    those next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can soc2doc read our auditor's comments or request status?">
    Not yet. Today the connection only uploads evidence. Comments and status
    stay in A-SCEND.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
